\section{Work \& Leadership Experience}

\cventry{INRIA Paris}%
  {Research Intern -- Machine Learning \& Symbolic Regression}{Jan.~2026 -- Jul.~2026}{%
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Designed and implemented \textbf{DragonSR}, a symbolic regression framework casting equation discovery as the optimisation of directed acyclic graphs (DAGs), built on top of DRAGON (Keisler et al., \textit{JMLR} 2024): multi-channel representation with sub-expression sharing, feature-selection operators, least-squares channel aggregation (sparse, nested, polynomial-rational), bandit-driven search (Mutant-UCB).
    \item Noise-robust pipeline: Gaussian-process denoising, stochastic subsampling, variable augmentation with XGBoost feature selection, correlation-based objective and structural complexity penalty.
    \item Benchmarked on 27 equations (Nguyen, 9 physical laws, 9 Sentinel-2 remote-sensing indices with 10+ input variables) against \textbf{PySR} (state of the art), over 4 noise levels $\times$ 10 runs: DragonSR recovers formulas up to 10\% noise where PySR recovers none (Hubble, ideal gas, Kepler, Leavitt, SAVI, EVI2\ldots).
    \item \textbf{First author} of \textit{DragonSR: Directed Acyclic Graph Search for Equation Discovery} (with J.~Keisler and A.~Charantonis), arXiv preprint forthcoming; code and results released (GitHub, Zenodo).
  \end{itemize}
}
